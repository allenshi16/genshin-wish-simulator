About Astral Wish Lab

Astral Wish Lab is a free, unofficial fan-made tool for Genshin Impact and Wuthering Waves players. Built by gamers who understand the frustration of spending primogems without knowing the odds, our wish simulator lets you rehearse pulls, track your pity, and plan your primogem budget — all in your browser, with no login required.

Our Mission

We believe players deserve transparent, accurate tools to make informed decisions about their pulls. That's why we built Astral Wish Lab: to provide a safe, free space to test wish mechanics, understand the pity system, and plan your primogem spending before committing real currency.

How It Works

Our simulator uses the exact probability model from the games: 0.6% base 5-star rate, soft pity from pull 74, hard pity at 90, 50/50 featured check with post-loss guarantee, and 10-wish 4-star hard pity. All calculations happen locally in your browser — we don't store your data or require an account.

Features

Astral Wish Lab includes character-event, weapon, and standard banner simulators, a pity tracker, primogem planner, collection log, and comprehensive guides on the pity system and primogem planning. We also cover Wuthering Waves with character builds, active codes, and banner schedules.
