About Astral Wish Lab

Astral Wish Lab is an unofficial, fan-made wish simulator and pity calculator inspired by the gacha system in Genshin Impact. It lets players test single or ten-pulls on original example banners, track separate pity and guarantee states, collect fictional results, and plan how many primogems they may need before a banner ends.

What the simulator does

Astral Wish Lab models the wish mechanics commonly seen in character-event, weapon-event, and standard banners. Each banner type uses its own pity counter, base 5-star rate, and guarantee logic so you can see how probability behaves over many pulls.

  • Character-event model: 0.6% base 5-star rate, 90-wish hard pity, featured 50/50 with a guarantee after a loss, and a 10-wish 4-star-or-higher guarantee.
  • Weapon-event model: 80-wish hard pity with a simplified one-point path.
  • Standard banner: A mixed pool with no featured 50/50.

Soft-pity ramp-up is included as an approximate curve. All example banners use an original fictional roster — no official character art, logos, or current game banner data are used.

How it works

Everything runs in your browser. Your simulated pulls, wish statistics, pity counters, and collection data are stored locally on your device. No account is required, no personal information is collected, and nothing about your pulls is uploaded to any server.

This means you can close the tab and come back later — your progress will still be there. You can also clear all local data at any time through your browser settings.

Who built this

Astral Wish Lab was built by Genshin Impact players who wanted a safe, ad-supported sandbox to test pull strategies and understand pity mechanics before spending primogems in-game. The project is maintained independently and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by HoYoverse or any of its partners.

We are not data miners and we do not leak official content. Example banners are deliberately fictional so that the tool remains a planning sandbox rather than a preview of upcoming game banners.

Editorial standards

Guides and character pages on Astral Wish Lab are written by players for players. Probability explanations follow publicly documented gacha mechanics. Where a value is an approximation (for example, the soft-pity curve), we say so explicitly rather than presenting it as an exact figure.
