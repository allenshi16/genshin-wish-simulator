How Xiao's banner pity works

Every featured character banner uses the same wish mechanics:

  • Hard pity: a five-star is guaranteed within 90 pulls.
  • Soft pity: the five-star rate rises sharply from pull 74.
  • 50/50: when a five-star drops, it's a 50% chance to be the featured character (Xiao).
  • Guarantee carries over: pity and the guarantee carry into the next featured banner, so pulls now still count toward a future Xiao banner.

Frequently asked questions about pulling for Xiao

  • How many pulls until hard pity for Xiao?

    Hard pity is 90 pulls. If you haven't pulled a five-star by your 89th wish, the 90th is guaranteed to be a five-star. Soft pity starts around pull 74, where the rate rises sharply.

  • Is Xiao guaranteed after losing the 50/50?

    Yes. If you lose the 50/50 on a featured character banner, your next five-star is guaranteed to be the featured character, and the guarantee carries into the next banner.

  • What are the odds of pulling Xiao?

    The base five-star rate is 0.6%, rising from pull 74 onward. When a five-star drops, you have a 50% chance it is the featured character (Xiao) and a 50% chance of a standard five-star.

  • Is Xiao a dps?

    Xiao is a 5-star Anemo Polearm user that plays a DPS role. Whether they are worth pulling depends on your roster and team archetypes.
