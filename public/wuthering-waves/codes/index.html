How to Redeem Wuthering Waves Codes

  1. Open Wuthering Waves and log into your account on any server.
  2. Open the menu by pressing Esc (PC) or the menu button (mobile).
  3. Go to Settings (gear icon) at the bottom of the menu.
  4. Click "Account" in the left sidebar.
  5. Click "Redeem Code" button.
  6. Enter the code exactly as shown (case-sensitive). No spaces.
  7. Click "Exchange" to claim your rewards.
  8. Check your inbox in-game to collect the rewards. They usually arrive within a few minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I redeem Wuthering Waves codes?

Open Wuthering Waves, go to Settings > Account > Redeem Code, enter the code, and claim your rewards. Codes are case-sensitive and may expire.

Where can I find new Wuwa codes?

New codes are released during livestreams, maintenance, and special events. Check this page weekly for the latest active codes.

Do Wuthering Waves codes expire?

Yes, most codes have an expiration date. Redeem them as soon as possible to avoid missing out on rewards.

Can I use codes on all servers?

Yes, codes typically work on all servers (NA, EU, Asia, etc.) unless otherwise specified.

Why isn't my code working?

Codes may be expired, already redeemed, or entered incorrectly. Double-check the spelling and ensure you haven't already used it on this account.
