How the Simulator Works

Real Probability Model

Uses the standard gacha probability: base 5-star rate, soft pity ramp, and hard pity guarantee. Results are simulated locally in your browser.

Pity Tracking

Tracks your pity counter across sessions. See exactly how many pulls until your next 5-star and whether you're in the soft pity zone.

50/50 Mechanics

Simulates the 50/50 check for featured characters. If you lose, the next 5-star is guaranteed — just like in the real game.

Wish History

Logs every pull with character name, rarity, and whether it was rate-up. Review your history and analyze your luck.

Why Use a Wish Simulator?

A wish simulator helps you:

  • Understand the odds: See how the pity system and 50/50 mechanics work in practice
  • Plan your pulls: Test how many pulls you need to guarantee a character
  • Practice budgeting: Use the primogem planner to see how many pulls you can afford before a banner ends
  • Have fun: Experience the thrill of pulling without spending real currency

Wuthering Waves vs Genshin Impact

Wuthering Waves and Genshin Impact use similar gacha mechanics:

  • Both have a base 5-star rate (approximately 0.6-0.7%)
  • Both use soft pity (increased rates after a certain number of pulls)
  • Both have hard pity (guaranteed 5-star at a specific pull count)
  • Both feature a 50/50 mechanic for featured characters
  • Pity carries over between banners in both games

Our simulator is based on the Genshin Impact model, which is very similar to Wuthering Waves. Use it to understand the general mechanics and plan your pulls accordingly.
