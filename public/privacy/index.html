What we collect

Astral Wish Lab is a browser-based tool. Your simulated pulls, wish statistics, pity counters, and collection data are stored only in your own browser (localStorage). We do not require an account, we do not ask for personal information, and nothing about your pulls is uploaded to us.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser localStorage to remember your simulator settings and statistics. This data never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time via your browser settings or by clearing site data.
