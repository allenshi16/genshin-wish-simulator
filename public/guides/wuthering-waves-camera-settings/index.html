Wuthering Waves Camera Settings

Camera Settings for Combat

For optimal combat performance, you want maximum awareness of enemy positions and attack patterns. Here's how to configure your camera:

  • Camera Height: Set to Medium-High or High. This gives you a better overhead view to see enemy telegraphs and avoid attacks.
  • Camera Sensitivity: 60-70% on PC, 50-60% on mobile. You need quick camera movement to track fast enemies but not so fast that you overshoot.
  • Camera Shake: Turn OFF or set to Low. Camera shake during ultimate animations and boss attacks can be disorienting and cause motion sickness.
  • Lock-On Camera: Enable lock-on camera smoothing. This keeps the enemy centered during combat without jarring movements.

Camera Settings for Exploration

When exploring Sol-3, you want a comfortable view that lets you appreciate the scenery while maintaining good visibility for platforming and combat encounters:

  • Camera Distance: Medium to Far. This gives you a wider view of the environment, helpful for spotting collectibles and planning routes.
  • Auto-Camera: On. Let the game automatically adjust the camera when climbing, gliding, or entering new areas.
  • Camera Sensitivity: Lower it slightly (40-50%) for more precise control when navigating tight spaces or platforming.

Reducing Motion Sickness

If you experience motion sickness while playing Wuthering Waves, try these adjustments:

  • Disable Camera Shake: This is the #1 cause of motion sickness in action games.
  • Lower Camera Sensitivity: Reduces the speed of camera movement, making it less jarring.
  • Increase FOV (if available): A wider field of view can reduce the "tunnel vision" effect that contributes to motion sickness.
  • Take Breaks: If you start feeling queasy, stop playing for 10-15 minutes.
  • Play in a Well-Lit Room: Reduces eye strain and helps your brain reconcile visual and vestibular inputs.

Platform-Specific Tips

PC

PC players have the most control over camera settings. Use a mouse for precise camera control, and consider binding camera reset to a convenient key. If you experience stuttering when moving the camera, check your frame rate and graphics settings.

Mobile

Mobile players should use touch controls with lower sensitivity to avoid overshooting. Consider using a controller if available for your device, as it provides more precise camera control.

PlayStation

DualSense controller provides excellent camera control on PS5. Adjust sensitivity in the game settings and consider using the adaptive trigger features for combat.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best camera height in Wuthering Waves?

Medium-High is recommended for most players. It provides good visibility in combat without being too far from your character.

How do I reduce camera shake?

Go to Settings > Camera > Camera Shake and set it to Off or Low. This reduces motion sickness during intense combat sequences.

Why does my camera feel laggy?

Camera lag can be caused by low frame rates or high sensitivity settings. Try lowering your graphics settings or reducing camera sensitivity.

Can I customize camera settings per character?

No, camera settings are global and apply to all characters. However, you can adjust sensitivity on the fly during gameplay.
