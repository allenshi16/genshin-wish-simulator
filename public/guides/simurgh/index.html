Genshin Simurgh: The Divine Bird of Vourukasha Oasis

Quick answer: Simurgh is the divine bird of Sumeru's desert lore, introduced in version 3.6. Born from a shard of Khvarena left by the Goddess of Flowers, she sacrificed herself in the Khaenri'ah cataclysm 500 years ago — her scattered light became the Pari and the flower sea of Vourukasha Oasis. Her story is played out in the world quest series Khvarena of Good and Evil.

Who is Simurgh in Genshin Impact?

Simurgh is a divine bird remembered in the lore of the Girdle of the Sands, the Sumeru desert region added in version 3.6. She is not a boss, not a playable character and not one of The Seven: she is a created being whose story survives in artifact text, quest dialogue and the landscape itself. In Vourukasha Oasis, the great tree Harvisptokhm marks the place where her legacy rests, and the little spirits called Pari — including your exploration companion Sorush and Zurvan, the First Pari — are her descendants. If you are here for the story rather than the rewards, the quest table below is the fastest route through it; if you are here for the 600 primogems, jump to the Amrita Pool section.

What was Simurgh before she became a divine bird?

She began as a shard of Khvarena — "the flower's wisdom, the sky's veins, the essence of life" — created by Nabu Malikata, the Goddess of Flowers. Foreseeing the abyssal tide that would follow her death, the Goddess entrusted the shard to Greater Lord Rukkhadevata with a promise: keep it safe until the black tide rises, then let it act. Rukkhadevata honored that promise by giving the shard a body. The in-game flavor text of the 3.6 artifact set Vourukasha's Glow recounts the moment (unofficial translation from the Chinese client text):

"Heed the Khvarena's wish, shape for it a wonderful body, transform it into a life of unmatched splendor — that was the divine bird Simurgh, gathering the colors of a thousand birds and the song of a thousand flowers."

So Simurgh is neither an Archon nor a primordial god: she is the Goddess of Flowers' last gift, given form by the Lord of Dendro. That origin is why the Pari regard her as a mother-figure rather than a deity, and why her power is described as light and purification rather than elemental authority.

What did Simurgh do during the cataclysm 500 years ago?

When the Khaenri'ah cataclysm broke, a bottomless rift opened in the desert and abyssal monsters poured through. The great tree Harvisptokhm sealed the rift, but the defilement remained. Egeria, the previous Hydro Archon, had died in the same disaster; her body became the sanctified amrita, pure water left behind in the oasis. Following Rukkhadevata's charge, Simurgh drank that amrita, fused her Khvarena power with it, and shattered her own divine form into countless Khvarena spirits. The scattered light cleansed the black tide and bloomed into the flower sea now called Vourukasha Oasis. The Khvarena that gained wisdom became the Pari; Zurvan, the first of them, gathered the rest and fought the abyss alongside Dainsleif and the survivors of Khaenri'ah. Exhausted Pari turn into Plumes of Purifying Light, and their memories return through the Amrita Pool to the Harvisptokhm.

Which quests tell Simurgh's story, and in what order?

The series Khvarena of Good and Evil unlocks automatically when you approach the teleport waypoint at Gavireh Lajavard in the Girdle of the Sands — no Adventure Rank gate beyond reaching the 3.6 map. Community guides agree on the dependency order: part one first, parts two and three in either order, then four and five. You receive Sorush, the Pari gadget, early in part one; she flies, clears purple mist and interacts with Khvarena gimmicks for the rest of the series.

What rewards does the Amrita Pool give?

The Amrita Pool (offering system "The Gift of Amrita") sits in a cave southwest of Vourukasha Oasis and opens after The Heart of Amrita. You offer Plumes of Purifying Light — the feathers left by exhausted Pari — in groups of six. There are 36 plumes in total, so six offerings; each blessing grants 100 primogems plus mora or talent materials, for 600 primogems overall, and completing all six awards the Sumeru: Amrita namecard. Offering 30 plumes (level 5) reveals the remaining plume locations on your map. Budget-wise, 600 primogems is 3.75 wishes at 160 each — small, but free: fold it into your primogems-per-patch plan, and check the pity system guide to see what 3.75 pulls actually buys you at your current pity. What music and real-world myths echo Simurgh?

Two tracks on the version 3.6 soundtrack album The Unfathomable Sand Dunes (HOYO-MiX, released April 2023) carry her name, both on disc 1, Sandstorm Remembrances: "Where Simurgh Rests" (track 5, composed and arranged by Peijia You, 1:21) and "Simurgh's Dream" (track 18, composed by Zhao Xin, 1:35). The name itself borrows from Persian mythology: the Simurgh of Iranian legend is a benevolent, healing bird — foster-mother of Zal in the Shahnameh and the goal of the birds' pilgrimage in Attar's Conference of the Birds. Community lore writers have long noted the parallel between that self-giving, world-witnessing bird and Genshin's sacrificed divine bird; HoYoverse has not published an official statement of intent, so treat the connection as a well-supported reading of the names and motifs, not confirmed design commentary.

Frequently asked questions

What is Simurgh in Genshin Impact?

A divine bird from Sumeru's 3.6 desert lore: formed from a Khvarena shard, sacrificed in the cataclysm 500 years ago, and the origin of the Pari and the Vourukasha Oasis flower sea.

What did the divine bird Simurgh originally used to be?

A shard of Khvarena created by the Goddess of Flowers (Nabu Malikata) and entrusted to Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, who gave it the body of a bird.

Is Simurgh a god or an Archon?

No — she is a created divine bird, not one of The Seven. The Pari, her descendants, do not refer to her as a god.

Can you meet or fight Simurgh in game?

No. She died 500 years before the present story. You encounter her legacy: Sorush, Zurvan, the Harvisptokhm, the oasis, and her story in the Vourukasha's Glow artifact text.

Which quest tells Simurgh's story?

Khvarena of Good and Evil (3.6): The Splendorous Sky That Day → Asipattravana Itihasa / Awakening's Real Sound → As the Khvarena's Light Shows → The Hymn of Tir Yazad, then The Heart of Amrita.

How many primogems does the Amrita Pool give?

600 total — six offerings of six Plumes of Purifying Light each, 100 primogems per blessing, plus the Sumeru: Amrita namecard at completion.

Which soundtrack tracks reference Simurgh?

"Where Simurgh Rests" and "Simurgh's Dream" on The Unfathomable Sand Dunes (3.6 OST, disc 1).
