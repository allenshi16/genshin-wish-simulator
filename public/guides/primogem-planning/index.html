How Many Wishes Per Patch? Genshin Primogems Per Patch

Quick answer: an active F2P Genshin Impact player earns about 7,700 primogems ≈ 48 wishes per patch (42-day version). One Welkin Moon card raises that to ~67 wishes; Welkin + Battle Pass reaches ~75 wishes. Since 160 primogems = 1 wish, the full source-by-source math below converts straight into pulls.

Is one patch of saving enough for a guaranteed 5-star?

Usually not. Expected pulls per 5-star are about 62, and the guaranteed-featured worst case is 180 pulls. At ~48 wishes per patch, F2P players need to save across two or more patches for a guaranteed featured character — which is exactly why planning matters. Frequently asked questions

How many wishes per patch as F2P in Genshin?

About 48 wishes per 42-day patch — roughly 7,700 primogems from dailies, events, Abyss, Theater and mail, at 160 primogems per wish. Big patches with lots of new quests can reach 56+.

How many primogems per patch as F2P?

Roughly 7,700–9,000 per 42-day patch from repeatable sources — about 48–56 wishes, before one-time quest and achievement rewards.

How many pulls does Welkin add per patch?

One Welkin card is 3,000 Genesis Crystals — about 18.75 wishes per patch if you buy one per cycle.

How many wishes is 1600 primogems?

Exactly 10 wishes at 160 primogems each.

Can F2P guarantee a featured character every patch?

No — a guaranteed featured 5-star can take up to 180 pulls, while F2P income is ~50 per patch. Save across multiple patches.
