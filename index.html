Genshin Wish Simulator and Pity Calculator

Astral Wish Lab is an unofficial fan-made multi-banner simulator. Test single or ten pulls on original character, weapon, and standard example banners, follow separate pity and guarantee states, collect fictional results, and plan how many primogems you may have before a banner ends.

Character guarantee, weapon path, and primogem planning

The character model uses a 0.6% base 5-star rate, 90-wish hard pity, a featured 50/50 with a guarantee after a loss, and a 10-wish 4-star-or-higher guarantee. The weapon example uses 80 hard pity and a simplified one-point path. Soft pity is explicitly approximate.

No login or game account is required. Wish history and the original fictional collection remain in this browser. Example banners do not represent current game data.
